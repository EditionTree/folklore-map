# Generated ornament variants

These are review assets only. They are shown at working interface sizes in
`/ornament-variants.html` and are not wired into the landing page.

The six images were created with the built-in image generator on a flat
magenta background, then converted locally to alpha PNGs. Files ending in
`-source.png` retain the chroma-key originals.

## Celtic frames

- `celtic-frame-v1.png`: continuous Insular manuscript ribbon, oak brown and
  antique gold, open 4:3 centre, no rectangular keyline.
- `celtic-frame-v2.png`: Pictish and Irish carved-stone interlace, heavier
  corner knots and narrower woven sides, open 4:3 centre.
- `celtic-frame-v3.png`: Arts and Crafts interlace with restrained oak-leaf
  terminals, open 4:3 centre.

All frame prompts required clear over-under crossings, a transparent-looking
empty centre, no square outline, no plaque, no text and no watermark.

## Realistic Celtic frames

- `celtic-frame-realistic-v1.png`: deeply carved aged English oak with visible
  grain, tool marks and dark waxed recesses.
- `celtic-frame-realistic-v2.png`: cast antique bronze with worn high points
  and restrained verdigris.
- `celtic-frame-realistic-v3.png`: weathered sandstone carving with softened
  edges and sparse traces of old gilding.

These prompts explicitly requested photorealistic museum-object materials,
heavier border proportions, clear over-under crossings and flat frontal light.

## Oak corners

- `oak-corner-v1.png`: Quercus robur copperplate botanical engraving with
  three deeply lobed leaves and two acorns.
- `oak-corner-v2.png`: British wood-engraving treatment with four oak leaves
  and an acorn pair.
- `oak-corner-v3.png`: illuminated-manuscript oak flourish with three leaves,
  hanging acorns and restrained orange details.

All oak prompts specified botanically recognisable English oak leaves,
rounded deep lobes, visible veins, acorns, an open diagonal corner composition,
no generic oval leaves, no frame, no text and no watermark.

## Oak 1 orientation family

- `oak-corner-upper-right.png`: upper-right entry, descending toward the left.
- `oak-corner-upper-left.png`: upper-left entry, arcing down toward the right.
- `oak-divider-horizontal.png`: low, wide branch intended as a section divider.
- `oak-edge-vertical.png`: tall narrow branch intended for a page edge.

All four use `oak-corner-v1.png` as the style reference and preserve its
copperplate hatching, muted sage wash, oak-brown ink and antique-gold acorns.

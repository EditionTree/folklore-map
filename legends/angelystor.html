Ghosts Llangernyw, Conwy, Wales

Angelystor

Angelystor, the Recording Angel, is a supernatural voice said to inhabit the churchyard of St Digain's Church in Llangernyw. Each Halloween night, the spirit announces the names of parishioners who will die in the coming year. The tradition is centred on the churchyard's ancient yew tree.

A visual interpretation of Angelystor speaking from the yew at St Digain's Church
The legend

At St Digain's Church in the Conwy village of Llangernyw stands a yew said to be among the oldest living things in Wales, and with it the tradition of Angelystor — the 'Recording Angel'. On Halloween night, and again on the eve of St John's, a solemn disembodied voice was said to be heard from within the church, naming aloud in Welsh those of the parish who would die in the coming year.

Each Halloween night, the spirit announces the names of parishioners who will die in the coming year.

The names from the churchyard

The best-known story tells of a sceptical local tailor, Siôn Ap Robert, who scoffed at the belief and went to the church on Halloween to disprove it — only to hear his own name called by the Angelystor. He is said to have protested that he was not ready, but died within the year as foretold. The legend bound the parish's reckoning of mortality to its ancient tree and churchyard, a yearly reminder voiced by an unseen angel in the dark.

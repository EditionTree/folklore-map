Avalon

The isle of eternal rest where Arthur was carried after his final battle — identified with Glastonbury by monks who claimed to have found his grave. Arthur sleeps here still, waiting to return when Britain needs him most. Many believe the monks lied about the grave. Few doubt the legend.

Awd Goggie

A shaggy caterpillar-spirit that Yorkshire parents summoned to keep children out of the orchard, said to swallow whole any child caught scrumping before the fruit was ripe.

The Legend

Awd Goggie is a nursery sprite of Yorkshire, conjured by country parents to keep children out of the orchards and fruit bushes before the crop was ready. Some tellings give him no fixed shape beyond a rustling presence in the leaves; others describe him as a monstrous caterpillar, swollen and bristling, able to move unseen through the branches and snatch any child caught scrumping among the leaves. Either way the threat did double duty: it protected the crop and kept youngsters from the bellyache of eating fruit before it ripened.

Its rustling presence in the leaves was enough to keep small hands at bay.

The Rustle in the Gooseberries

He has no grand legend, only the vivid threat of his presence, one of a small army of cautionary bogeys, like the Gooseberry Wife of the Isle of Wight or Lazy Lawrence, that did the practical work of guarding a crop and keeping children out of danger. Such spirits, slight as they seem, preserve a whole vanished landscape of folk belief in which every orchard, pond and dark corner had its watchful guardian.

Deities Gwynedd, Wales

Blodeuwedd

Created from nine flowers — broom, meadowsweet and oak among them — as a wife for the hero Lleu, who could not marry a mortal. She betrayed him with a lover and conspired to kill him. As punishment the wizard Gwydion turned her into an owl, and since that day all other birds mob the owl wherever they find it.

Brahan Seer

Brahan, Ross-shire

Coinneach Odhar, remembered as the Brahan Seer, is a Highland prophetic figure said to have possessed the Second Sight. Folklore associates him with the Brahan estate and with predictions about the Seaforth family and later Scottish events.

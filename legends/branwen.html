Legendary Figures Anglesey, Wales

Branwen

BRAHN-wen

Daughter of Llyr and sister of Bran the Blessed, Branwen was given in marriage to the King of Ireland and reduced to working in the kitchens. She trained a starling to carry a message to her brother; he came with all the warriors of Britain, and the war that followed destroyed both islands.

A visual interpretation of Branwen sending her message across the sea
The Legend

Branwen, daughter of Llŷr, is the tragic heroine of the Second Branch of the Mabinogi, sister to the giant-king Bran the Blessed. She was given in marriage to Matholwch, King of Ireland, to bind the two lands in peace; but the peace was broken when her half-brother Efnysien, slighted, maimed the Irish horses, and though Bran made amends with the gift of a magical cauldron that revived the dead, the insult festered.

She tamed a starling, taught it speech, and sent it across the sea with word of her misery.

The Message Across the Water

In Ireland Branwen was driven from the king's bed and set to drudge in the kitchens, her ear boxed daily by the butcher. She tamed a starling, taught it speech, and sent it across the sea with word of her misery; Bran led a host to Ireland to avenge her, and in the terrible war that followed nearly all on both sides were slain, Bran himself mortally wounded. Branwen returned to Wales only to die of a broken heart on the bank of the Alaw in Anglesey, where a mound long known as Bedd Branwen was held to be her grave.

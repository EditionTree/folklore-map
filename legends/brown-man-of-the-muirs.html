Brown Man of the Muirs

Northumberland

A small, fierce guardian spirit of the Border moors, protector of moorland animals from hunters who killed more than they needed. He appeared in terrible form to warn the greedy. Those who ignored him met with accidents, madness, or worse before they reached home.

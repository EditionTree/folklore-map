Beasts

Britain and Ireland teem with monstrous beasts, among them the spectral black dogs of lonely lanes, the water-horses that drag riders down to drowning, and the territorial 'worms' and wild cattle of older belief. Many are bound to a single lake, moor or parish, where the creature guards a boundary or warns of a coming death.

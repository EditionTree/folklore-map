Deities

Older gods linger in the landscape, among them the Dagda and the Morrígan of Irish myth, Brigid and Lugh, the Brythonic figures of the Mabinogi, and the Romano-British deities of spring and grove. Their names survive in rivers, hills and holy wells long after their worship faded.

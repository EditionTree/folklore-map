Fae & Spirits

The fair folk of British and Irish tradition are neither tiny nor harmless: the Irish sídhe of the hollow hills, the Welsh Tylwyth Teg, the household brownies and boggarts, and the changelings left in a stolen child's place. Encounters turn on courtesy, theft, and time that runs strangely beneath the hill.

Ghosts

The ghosts of these islands are rarely vague. They are the wronged revenant who will not stay buried, the screaming skull that cannot be moved, the phantom coach, and the weeping or radiant figure tied to a named hall or churchyard — stories that preserve old anxieties about unquiet death, broken oaths and the boundary between the living and the dead.

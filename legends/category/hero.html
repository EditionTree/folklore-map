Legendary Figures

The legendary figures of these islands range from Arthur and his knights to Fionn mac Cumhaill and the Fianna, from Robin Hood to the saints and outlaws whose deeds are still tied to named places. History and folklore blur: many were real, yet tradition gave them sleeping caves, buried treasure and returns foretold.

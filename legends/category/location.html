Sacred Sites

Some places gathered folklore for thousands of years — stone circles said to be dancers turned to stone, holy wells sought for healing, barrows that must not be disturbed, and hills where a king and his knights are said to sleep. These are the sites where the supernatural was felt to press closest to the land.

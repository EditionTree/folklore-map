Sacred Sites

Some places gathered folklore for thousands of years, from stone circles said to be dancers turned to stone to holy wells sought for healing, barrows that must not be disturbed, and hills where a king and his knights are said to sleep. These are the sites where the supernatural was felt to press closest to the land.

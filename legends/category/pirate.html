Pirates

Beyond the documented history, British and Irish coasts kept a folklore of pirates, privateers and wreckers — figures like Grace O'Malley and the smugglers of the Cornish and Essex shores, remembered in ballad and treasure-lore and in tales of false lights luring ships onto the rocks.

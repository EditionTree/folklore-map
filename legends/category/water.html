Aquatic Legends

Water holds its own people and its own perils — mermaids and selkies, the kelpie and each-uisge of the lochs, drowned bells and whole kingdoms sunk beneath the waves. These are the traditions of communities living by lake, river and a sea that both fed them and took them.

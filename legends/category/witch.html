Witches

From the trial records of North Berwick and Pendle to the cunning-folk and charmers of village life, the witch-lore of these islands mixes real persecution with belief in the evil eye, shape-shifting hares and butter that would not churn. Many places still carry the name of a woman who was accused, drowned or burned.

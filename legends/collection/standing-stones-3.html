Standing Stones & Stone Circles

The prehistoric monuments of Britain and Ireland gathered folklore for thousands of years after they were raised. Circles said to be dancers or maidens turned to stone, megaliths that cannot be counted, stones that walk to drink at midnight — the traditions preserve a folk memory of the monuments as living, watchful and not to be meddled with.

Sacred SitesThe PipersCornwallThe Pipers are a pair of standing stones near The Hurlers stone circles, located on Bodmin Moor near the village of Minions, Cornwall, UK. They share the name with another pair of standing stones near the Merry Maidens to the south of the village of St Buryan, also in Cornwall. Sacred SitesTimoney StonesTimoney, County TipperaryOver a hundred standing stones scattered across a Tipperary hilltop in no clear pattern — local tradition calls the central flat stone a ‘Druid’s altar’ and holds that men of note lie buried beneath every one. Sacred SitesTownleyhall passage graveTownleyhall, County LouthA 5,000-year-old passage tomb in the Brú na Bóinne complex — its entrance aligns with midsummer sunrise, linking it to the same Otherworld mythology as neighbouring Newgrange and Dowth. Sacred SitesTwelve Apostles, West YorkshireYorkshireThe Twelve Apostles is a stone circle near Ilkley and Burley in Wharfedale in West Yorkshire, England. Sacred SitesWaun MawnPreseli Hills, Pembrokeshire, WalesDismantled bluestones from this Preseli mountain circle may be the very stones Merlin carried to build Stonehenge — a twelfth-century legend now backed by startling archaeological evidence. Sacred SitesWhetstonesWelshpool, Powys, WalesA destroyed stone circle beneath Corndon Hill on the Welsh Marches — one of three sacred rings guarding the slopes where, legend says, a witch was turned to stone for milking a fairy cow dry. Sacred SitesWithypool Stone CircleSomersetA ring of nearly a hundred small stones on Exmoor moorland — where the pixies come to thresh at harvest, and where no one has ever counted the stones twice and arrived at the same number.

Cú Roí mac Dáire

Cú Roí mac Dáire is a supernatural warrior-king of Munster in Irish tradition. His stronghold is associated with Caherconree in the Slieve Mish Mountains, where legend says his fortress revolved at night so that enemies could not find its entrance.

Donn Fírinne

DON FEE-rin-yeh

Donn Fírinne is the fairy king of Knockfierna, the Hill of Truth, in County Limerick. Local tradition says he rides a white horse through storm clouds and punishes attempts to disturb the hill's fairy forts with violent weather.

A visual interpretation of Donn Fírinne
The Legend

Donn Fírinne is the fairy king of Knockfierna — Cnoc Fírinne, the 'Hill of Truth' — a striking isolated hill in County Limerick long held to be one of the great fairy seats of Munster. From his palace within the hill Donn was said to rule the surrounding country's Good People, and to ride out on a fine white horse, especially before a death in the district or to gather in the souls of the dead.

The Otherworld at the Heart of the Tale

He was bound up too with the weather: when clouds gathered on the summit of Knockfierna the people said 'Donn is gathering the clouds for rain', and a clear hill meant fair weather, so that the fairy king doubled as a kind of local weather-god. This Munster Donn is widely thought to be a survival of the older Donn of Irish myth, the lord of the dead whose house, Tech Duinn, lay off the south-west coast and to whom the souls of the dead first gathered. At Knockfierna the ancient god of the dead lingered into recent centuries as a fairy king riding the hill on his white horse.

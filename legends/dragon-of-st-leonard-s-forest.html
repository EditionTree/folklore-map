Dragon of St Leonard's Forest

Sussex

For many years a great serpent made its lair in the dense forest south of Horsham, ravaging cattle and poisoning the land. Witnesses in 1614 described it as nine feet long, leaving a trail of slime and a lingering stench. Where its body coiled, white lilies and adder's tongue sprang up — plants said to have grown from its shed venom.

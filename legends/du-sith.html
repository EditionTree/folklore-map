Fae & Spirits Isle of Islay, Scotland

Du Sith

The Black Elf of Islay — a wholly malevolent fairy of the Inner Hebrides, said to haunt the moorland and coastal margins of the island. Unlike the mischievous fairy folk, the Du Sith bears nothing but hostility toward any human it encounters.

The legend

Du Sith — the 'Black Elf' or Dubh Sìth — is a malevolent fairy of the Inner Hebrides associated with the island of Islay, but he is best remembered for his part in a historical battle dressed in legendary clothing. In the tradition of the Battle of Traigh Ghruinneard (Gruinart) in 1598, fought between rival branches of Clan Donald and the Macleans, Du Sith appears as a sinister black-clad archer of supernatural skill.

The story

The story tells that Sir Lachlan Mór Maclean of Duart, the giant chief of the Macleans, was killed not by an honest warrior but by this Black Elf, who shot him dead with an arrow from concealment — a fairy hand deciding the day. Whether understood as a real outcast bowman of fearsome reputation or as a genuine fairy of the moor and shore taking a hand in mortal affairs, Du Sith embodies the dark, dangerous side of Hebridean fairy belief, where the otherworld could reach into the bloodiest events of clan history.

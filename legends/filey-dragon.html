Dragons

Filey Dragon

North Yorkshire

A fearsome dragon that made its lair in the tidal gully of Filey Brigg. The townsfolk defeated it by luring it to eat so much sticky parkin cake that its jaws seized shut and it plunged into the sea to drown. The jagged rocks of the Brigg are said to be its bones, jutting into the North Sea still.

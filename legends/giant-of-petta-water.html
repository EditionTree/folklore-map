Giant of Petta Water

Petta Water and Kneefell, Shetland Mainland, Scotland

A Shetland giant once lived near Petta Water alongside trows who tormented him until he tried to carry them away in a kishie basket and throw them into the sea. The weight of the trows broke the basket, scattering them across the landscape. Local tradition says the giant's footprint remains as Petta Water and the hollow where he set down his knee survives in Kneefell.

Hill of Tara

The ancient seat of the High Kings of Ireland, where sovereignty itself was tested by the Lia Fáil — the Stone of Destiny that cried out beneath the rightful king. The Banquet Hall could seat the whole of Ireland. Below it the Tuatha Dé Danann built their great mound. It is the spiritual centre of Ireland.

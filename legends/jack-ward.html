Pirates

Jack Ward

Faversham, Kent

The Faversham-born corsair Jack Ward became England's notorious Arch-Pirate. Plays, pamphlets and ballads followed him in his own lifetime. The traditional song Captain Ward and the Rainbow casts him as a king of the sea who defeats the royal ship sent against him.

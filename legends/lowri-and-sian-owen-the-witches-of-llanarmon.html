Lowri and Siân Owen, the Witches of Llanarmon

Two sisters near Pwllheli ran a cottage "school" where they cast out unclean spirits by ceremony and could read the sea's ebb and flow before it turned.

The Legend

Two sisters near Pwllheli ran a cottage "school" where they cast out unclean spirits by ceremony and could read the sea's ebb and flow before it turned.

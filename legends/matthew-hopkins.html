Witches

Matthew Hopkins

Essex

Self-styled Witch Finder General, Matthew Hopkins conducted a brutal campaign across East Anglia between 1644 and 1647, sending hundreds to trial and over a hundred to the gallows. He claimed authority from Parliament and charged by the accused, growing rich on fear. His own death was swiftly followed by rumours that he had been tried by his own methods and hanged.

Miosgán Meadhbha

Knocknarea, County Sligo

Miosgán Meadhbha, anglicized Miosgan Meva and also called Maeve's Cairn, is a large cairn on the summit of Knocknarea in County Sligo, Ireland. It is thought to conceal a passage tomb from the Neolithic. It is the largest cairn in Ireland, excepting those at Brú na Bóinne in Meath.

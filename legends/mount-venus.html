Origins & Dating

Approximate Originc. 3800-3400 BC
Historical PeriodNeolithic Ireland (construction); collapse date genuinely uncertain, possibly 18th century if the earthquake theory holds
Earliest Written RecordDescribed by the antiquarian William Borlase (18th century) as 'one of the most magnificent in the world'; the monument's collapse has been speculatively linked by some sources to the 1755 Lisbon earthquake (whose effects were felt across the British Isles) or an earlier 1690 earthquake, though this causal link is unconfirmed

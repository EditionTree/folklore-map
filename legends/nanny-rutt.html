Nanny Rutt

Nanny Rutt is a character in a cautionary tale associated with Nanny Rutt's well, an artesian spring in Math Wood, near Northorpe, in the parish of Thurlby, Lincolnshire. The story goes that a named girl went into the wood, to the well and disappeared having been taken off by Nanny Rutt.

The Legend

Nanny Rutt is the witch or hag of a Lincolnshire cautionary tale, haunting Math Wood near Northorpe, where a spring called Nanny Rutt's Well still bears her name. The story is told to warn against straying: a young woman, slipping out at dusk to meet her sweetheart who never comes, wanders deeper into the darkening wood and meets an old woman who offers her shelter in a ruined cottage.

The Story

What becomes of the girl varies in the telling — in the bleakest versions she is never seen again, taken or destroyed by Nanny Rutt — but the moral is constant: the wood after nightfall is no place for a girl alone, and the strange old woman of the trees is not to be trusted. Like Peg Powler at the river or the Gooseberry Wife in the garden, Nanny Rutt is one of the local 'fearsome' figures conjured to fence off a real danger with a memorable dread, and her well keeps the warning rooted in the Lincolnshire landscape.

Early Medieval Britain and Ireland

Roughly 600 to 1066, covering Anglo-Saxon England, early Christian Ireland, and the Pictish and Gaelic kingdoms of Scotland. These are the first centuries whose stories survive in something close to their own words. Irish monastic scribes wrote down the Ulster and Mythological cycles, and Old English poetry preserves wyrms, barrow-wights and night-monsters that were already old when the poems were made. Kings, saints and heroes of these centuries still have hills, wells and stones named after them across all three countries.

Georgian Britain

1714 to 1837, the era of the Enlightenment, the agricultural and early industrial revolutions, and a golden age of smuggling along Britain and Ireland's coasts. Antiquarian interest in the earlier past began in earnest during this period, alongside the first systematic folklore collecting.

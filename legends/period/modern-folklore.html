Modern Folklore

From the First World War to the present — a period often assumed to be free of folklore, but which has in fact produced its own rich seam of urban legend, roadside ghost sightings, cryptid reports and reinterpreted older tradition. Modern media, from newspapers to the internet, now do much of the work oral transmission once did.

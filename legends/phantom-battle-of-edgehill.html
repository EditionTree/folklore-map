Phantom Battle of Edgehill

Edgehill, Warwickshire

After the Battle of Edgehill in 1642, witnesses reported ghostly armies replaying the fighting above the battlefield. The tradition includes apparitions, battle cries, cannon fire and the sound of horses around the anniversary of the clash.

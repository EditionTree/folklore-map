Legendary Figures Pembrokeshire, Wales

Pryderi

Son of Pwyll and Rhiannon, the only figure to appear in all four branches of the Mabinogion. Snatched at birth by a monstrous hand, raised in a horse-lord's stable, and finally killed in single combat by Gwydion after a war over stolen pigs. His whole life is the thread on which the Welsh mythological cycle hangs.

The legend

Pryderi son of Pwyll and Rhiannon is the one figure who appears in all four branches of the Mabinogi, binding the great medieval Welsh prose cycle together. He is born at Dyfed and vanishes from his cradle the same night, snatched by a monstrous clawed arm; far away, the horse-lord Teyrnon cuts off the arm to save a foal, finds the child, and rears him until his likeness to Pwyll reveals him. Restored to his mother Rhiannon — falsely accused of his murder — he is given the name Pryderi, 'care' or 'anxiety'.

The story

As a man he rules Dyfed, befriends Manawydan, and suffers the enchantment that lays his land waste in the third branch, when he and his mother are trapped at a magical fortress. In the fourth branch he is drawn into war with Gwynedd over a gift of otherworldly swine and is killed in single combat by the magician Gwydion, by magic as much as by strength. His life, from the stolen cradle to the enchanted ending, carries the deep folk-motifs of the lost and recovered child that run through the whole Mabinogi.

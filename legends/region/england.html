Folklore of England

English folklore is a patchwork of county traditions — the black dogs of East Anglia, the dragons and 'worms' of the north, the boggarts and witches of Lancashire, and the green men and well-dressings of the older calendar. Much of it is rooted in a single parish, stone or stretch of road.

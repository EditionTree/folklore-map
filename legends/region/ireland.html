Folklore of Ireland

Irish tradition is dense with the Tuatha Dé Danann and the sídhe of the hollow hills, the banshee's warning, the holy wells and their saints, and the heroes of the Ulster and Fenian cycles — the supernatural mapped onto raths, cairns and the great prehistoric monuments of the Boyne.

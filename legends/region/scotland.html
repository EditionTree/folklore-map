Folklore of Scotland

Scottish tradition runs from the Highland water-horses and the Cailleach who shapes the mountains to the selkies and trows of Orkney and Shetland, the brownies of the farm, and the witch trials of the Lowlands. Gaelic and Norse belief meet across its glens and islands.

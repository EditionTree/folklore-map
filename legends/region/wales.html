Folklore of Wales

Welsh folklore is steeped in the Mabinogi and the Arthurian past — the red dragon of Dinas Emrys, the lake maidens of the Tylwyth Teg, the fairy cattle of the mountains, and the Mari Lwyd of midwinter. So many of its tales are anchored to a named lake, hill or holy well.

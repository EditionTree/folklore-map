Folklore of Wales

Welsh folklore leans heavily on the Mabinogi, the medieval collection of tales that still shapes how the country tells its own myths. Arthur belongs here too, and so does the red dragon said to have fought beneath the hill at Dinas Emrys before it ever appeared on a flag. Water carries its own stories. A lake maiden might marry a farmer at Llyn y Fan Fach and vanish the moment he breaks his promise. Fairy cattle graze mountain pasture that no one else can find. Every midwinter the Mari Lwyd, a horse's skull draped in a white sheet, still goes door to door demanding entry in verse.

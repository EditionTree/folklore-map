St Dwynwen

Llanddwyn Island, Anglesey

St Dwynwen is the Welsh patron saint of lovers. Her legend is associated with Llanddwyn Island, where the ruins of her church and a holy well became a place of pilgrimage and a focus for traditions about love and fortune.

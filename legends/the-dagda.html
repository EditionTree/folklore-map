Deities

The Dagda

County Meath, Ireland

The Good God of Irish mythology — father-figure of the Tuatha Dé Danann, keeper of the inexhaustible cauldron from which none went hungry, wielder of a club so vast it took eight men to carry. One end killed the living; the other raised the dead. He feasted and loved without shame and ruled with tremendous power.

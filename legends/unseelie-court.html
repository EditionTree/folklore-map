Fae & Spirits

Unseelie Court

Scotland

The dark host of Scottish fairy lore — malevolent fae who needed no reason to harm mortals. They flew through the winter night as the Sluagh, pelting travellers with fairy shot. No offering could appease them. They are the reason you do not go out alone after dark in the Lowlands.

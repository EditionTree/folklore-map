Veasta

Orkney

A sea beast of Orcadian waters, seen offshore during storms in old accounts. Like many Norse-influenced sea legends of Orkney, Veasta sits between monster and natural phenomenon — a reminder that the sea around these islands has always been treated as a living, sentient thing.

Privacy Notice

Last updated: June 2026

The Folklore Map of the British Isles (“we”, EditionTree) respects your privacy. This notice explains the limited data we collect and why. We do not require accounts, and we do not ask for your name or email anywhere on the site.

Bug reports

If you submit a bug report, we collect: the text you write (the description and steps), the page address you were viewing, your browser’s user-agent string (which identifies your browser and operating system), and whether you are on a mobile or desktop device. We do not collect your name, email, account, or IP address. Please do not include personal details in the report text.

Why: our legitimate interest in diagnosing and fixing faults on the site. Retention: bug reports are automatically deleted 30 days after they have been actioned.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used — such as which pages are viewed, approximate location, and device type — so we can improve it. Google sets cookies and processes this data as described in Google’s privacy policy.

Who processes your data

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Bug reports are stored using Supabase (our database provider). Analytics is provided by Google. These providers act as our data processors.

Your rights & contact

You may request access to, or deletion of, any personal data we hold. Because bug reports are anonymous, please describe the report (its rough date and topic) so we can locate it. Contact us at: folkloremap.conjoined774@passinbox.com
